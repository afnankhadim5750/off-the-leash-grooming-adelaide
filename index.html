Why grooming matters

What good grooming looks like

This is what a good groomer offers any dog, in general: steady, patient handling, proper coat care, and a clean finish your dog can be comfortable with.

  • Gentle handling

    Dogs do best when grooming is calm and unhurried, with breaks when a dog needs them.

  • Coat care

    Regular washing, brushing and clipping keeps coats clean, tangle-free and easier to manage at home.

  • A clean finish

    A tidy face, neat paws and cared-for nails leave a dog comfortable, clean and ready for the week.
